For a weekend in Blue Ridge, GA, pack layers for unpredictable mountain weather (fleece, rain jacket), sturdy hiking shoes, and comfortable casual wear for downtown. Essential items include a daypack for hiking, sunscreen, bug spray, a camera, and a swimsuit for cabin hot tubs or water activities. Don't forget, it's known as the Trout Capital of Georgia. 

Outdoor Gear & Hiking List
Backpack: Small daypack for water bottles, snacks, and gear.
Footwear: Sturdy, broken-in hiking boots or shoes.
Layers: Moisture-wicking t-shirts, fleece/hoodie for cool evenings, and a windbreaker or rain poncho.
Accessories: Sunscreen (SPF 15+), sunglasses, bug spray, and hats.
Trekking Poles: Helpful for navigating steep/uneven trails.
Safety: Flashlight/headlamp and a small first aid kit. 

Relaxing List    (Yes it is an activity that I enjoy greatly.)
Relaxation: Comfortable clothing, slippers, and a robe.
Cozy Extras: Extra blankets, firewood, and fixings for s'mores.
Entertainment: Board games, cards, or a book.
Food: Eat out or do some home style cooking.
Reusable Shopping Bag: For visiting downtown shops.
Water Activities: Water shoes for tubing/kayaking.
Town/Dining: Casual clothes and comfortable shoes for exploring Main Street shops and local breweries. 
Attitude: you don't need much else if you just come to relax and enjoy it up here.
