This Short-Term Rental Agreement (“Agreement”) is between the Weeks Creek Haven property owner or authorized manager (“Owner”) and the primary guest named above (“Guest”). By accepting electronically, Guest confirms that the reservation details are correct, agrees to conduct this transaction electronically, and accepts responsibility for everyone in the Guest’s party.

  1. Reservation, charges, and payment

    An automatically applied Early Bird price is held for seven days after approval. Guest must accept this Agreement and pay the required deposit during that period to keep the discounted price. The normal 24-hour deposit deadline does not apply to an approved Early Bird rate. If either step is incomplete, the discount expires and the stay must be repriced or rebooked at the then-current rate. Once completed on time, the approved price remains with the booking. The reservation covers only the dates, registered guests, and vehicles disclosed in the booking. The reservation becomes final when this Agreement is accepted and the required payment is made. Unless the stay is complimentary or Owner approves different written terms, a reservation deposit equal to 20% of the approved pre-tax stay total is due within 24 hours after approval, and the remaining balance is due seven days before check-in. For a reservation approved within seven days of check-in, payment in full is due upon approval. If payment is late, Owner will send one reminder and allow a 24-hour grace period; if payment remains unpaid, Owner may release the dates. The invoice separately identifies the stay charges, approved discounts, cleaning or pet charges, optional services, taxes, government fees, and the $300 refundable security deposit. Guest authorizes only the charges disclosed in the reservation, this Agreement, or a later written approval. The $300 security deposit is not part of the reservation deposit and is not taxed. It is normally refunded to the original payment method within seven days after checkout, less documented charges allowed under this Agreement.

  2. Cancellation and refunds

    Guest receives a 100% refund of amounts paid when written cancellation notice is received by Owner at least 24 hours before the scheduled 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time check-in. For notice received inside 24 hours, Owner retains the reservation deposit, equal to 20% of the approved pre-tax stay total, and refunds any remaining amount paid. A cancellation is complete when Owner receives the notice and sends confirmation. If Owner must cancel because the property is unavailable and cannot provide the stay, Owner will refund all amounts paid. Owner may grant a written exception or goodwill refund but is not required to do so. Payment-processor timing controls when approved refunds appear on Guest’s account.

  3. Occupancy and permitted use

    The cabin is for one private residential vacation party only. No parties, commercial events, weddings, ticketed gatherings, commercial activity, subletting, or unregistered overnight guests are permitted. Occupancy may never exceed the registered count or the property limit of 11 people. Guest must be at least 25 years old, be the person whose name appears on the reservation, stay at the property for the reservation, provide accurate contact and billing information, and supervise all minors. Owner may request a matching government-issued photo ID before releasing private access information.

  4. Arrival, departure, and access

    Standard check-in begins at 4:00 p.m. and standard checkout is 11:00 a.m. The optional $50 late-checkout service extends checkout to noon only when purchased and confirmed by Owner. Guest will follow the arrival, parking, access, and checkout instructions in the welcome packet and Weeks Creek Guest Guide. Unless Owner agrees otherwise in writing, early entry or any later departure is not guaranteed. Guest will secure doors and windows when leaving and return keys or access devices as directed.

  5. House care, pets, septic, and trash

    No smoking or vaping is permitted inside the cabin. Pets are permitted only when disclosed, approved, and included in the reservation; Guest is responsible for pet waste, damage, and applicable pet charges. Guest will keep the property reasonably clean, use plumbing and the septic system only as directed, secure all outdoor trash in the designated bear-resistant containers, and complete the checkout checklist. Furniture, electronics, linens, and supplies must remain at the property.

  6. Parking, neighbors, and local rules

    All vehicles must remain in the designated parking area within the property boundaries. No vehicle may block the road, road right-of-way, driveway access, or neighboring property. Guest and the party will respect posted boundaries and comply with Fannin County noise, lighting, Habitat and Environmental Protection, emergency, and other applicable rules. Instructions from Owner or the designated local contact concerning safety or a verified complaint must be followed promptly.

  7. Safety, creek, hot tub, fire, and wildlife

    Mountain terrain, steps, rocks, the creek and river, wildlife, weather, hot tub, fireplace, grill, and outdoor firepit involve inherent risks. Guest will supervise children, avoid glass near the hot tub, follow posted equipment instructions, obey burn bans, never leave fire unattended, and avoid feeding or approaching wildlife. Fireworks, explosives, firearm discharge, and uncontained fires are prohibited. Fires are allowed only in provided equipment and designated areas when lawful and safe. Alcohol, medication, health conditions, and weather can increase these risks.

  8. Emergency and county notices

    Guest will review the posted Fannin County guest notice, maximum occupancy, 911 address, emergency contacts, nearest-hospital information, and evacuation guidance upon arrival. In an emergency, call 911 first. Guest will comply with any evacuation, reentry, burn-ban, severe-weather, or other government order and will promptly notify Owner of an emergency or unsafe condition when it is safe to do so.

  9. Damage and problems

    Guest will promptly report unsafe conditions, damage, equipment failures, or maintenance problems and allow reasonable access for urgent repairs. Guest is responsible for the actual cost of damage, excessive cleaning, missing property, or rule violations caused by Guest or the party, beyond ordinary wear and tear. Owner will document and itemize any deduction from the refundable security deposit. Costs exceeding the deposit remain Guest’s responsibility.

  10. Condition, interruptions, and remedies

    Guest accepts that rural utilities, internet, weather, roads, and amenities can experience interruptions. Owner will make reasonable efforts to correct material problems reported during the stay. Any discount, credit, relocation, or partial refund for a problem must be agreed to by Owner in writing; this does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

  11. Assumption of risk and responsibility

    To the extent permitted by Georgia law, Guest voluntarily assumes the ordinary and inherent risks of using the cabin, property, creek area, hot tub, fires, stairs, decks, mountain roads, and outdoor surroundings. Guest is responsible for personal property and is encouraged to obtain travel insurance. Guest will reimburse Owner for documented loss, claims, or reasonable costs caused by Guest’s or the party’s negligent, intentional, unlawful, or materially breaching conduct, to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in this Agreement releases a party from liability that cannot legally be waived.

  12. Default and removal

    A material violation of this Agreement, dangerous or unlawful conduct, unauthorized occupancy, or refusal to protect the property may result in termination of the stay as permitted by law. No refund of stay charges is owed for removal caused by Guest’s material breach, subject to nonwaivable law. The refundable security deposit remains subject to documented deductions and the refund process above.

  13. Electronic records; entire agreement

    Guest consents to electronic records and signatures and may print or save this page. This Agreement, the confirmed reservation details, written payment terms, and written house instructions form the agreement between the parties. Changes or exceptions must be in a writing accepted by Owner. If one provision is unenforceable, the remainder continues. Georgia law governs, and any dispute will be brought in a court with jurisdiction in Fannin County, Georgia, unless applicable law requires otherwise.
