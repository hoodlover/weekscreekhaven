Cell service can fade in the Aska area. Load the route before leaving downtown Blue Ridge.

Parking

The cabin has three to four flat parking spaces. Please keep the drive clear for emergency access.

Connection

High-speed fiber Wi-Fi is available at the cabin. Private door and Wi-Fi details stay in your Weeks Creek Guest Guide.

Mountain safety: Call 911 for emergencies. Keep food and trash secured from wildlife, supervise children near the creek and slopes, and observe all current burn restrictions. Before heading into the mountains, add the Guest Guide web app to your phone so your door code, directions, cabin details, and other essentials remain available even when cell service disappears.
