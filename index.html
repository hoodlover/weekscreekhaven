Creekside in the Aska Adventure Area

A real cabin with room to exhale.

Weeks Creek Haven is a three-level log cabin tucked into a wooded hollow outside Blue Ridge. It is comfortable without feeling precious, private without feeling remote, and close enough to the river, trails, and town to make every kind of mountain day possible.

Come with your people. Cook a proper meal, claim a porch chair, listen to the creek, and decide whether the hot tub counts as an evening plan. We believe it does.

Your own little hollow

Close to Blue Ridge. Closer to the creek.

The cabin sits in the Aska Adventure Area, where the roads narrow, the cell bars disappear, and the mountains start doing what you came for. Downtown Blue Ridge is still within reach, but back at the cabin the soundtrack is mostly water and trees.

  • On the propertyWeeks Creek, a private footbridge, porch seating, a firepit, and a hot tub beneath the trees.
  • Down the roadToccoa River access, trout water, waterfalls, hiking, tubing, and the Aska trails.
  • In townRestaurants, shops, groceries, and the Blue Ridge Scenic Railway.
