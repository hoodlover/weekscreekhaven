What’s Worth Bringing

Mountain weather likes to improvise. Pack layers, sturdy shoes, and whatever helps you settle in. Save maps while you still have good service, and pick up groceries before heading deep into the Aska Adventure Area. Everything after that is mostly gravy. And if all that talk of gravy has you craving dinner, remember: nobody is DASHing to your DOOR around here. BYOF—Bring Your Own Food.

Trail Days

  • Wear: Broken-in hiking boots or sturdy trail shoes, layers, and a rain shell.
  • Carry: A small daypack, refillable water bottle, snacks, sunscreen, bug spray, and a hat.
  • Nice to have: Trekking poles, a headlamp, a small first-aid kit, and an offline map.

River & Fishing

  • Bring: A swimsuit, water shoes, quick-dry clothes, and a dry bag for phones and keys.
  • If fishing: Pack your gear and arrange the proper Georgia fishing license before casting.
  • Back at the cabin: Dedicated hot-tub towels are already waiting in the entertainment room.

Cabin Time

  • Get comfortable: Soft layers, slippers, and a robe if that is your particular brand of luxury.
  • Slow down: Bring a book, favorite game, cards, or nothing at all. The creek is fairly entertaining.
  • Food: The kitchen is well stocked for cooking; bring your groceries, drinks, and favorite snacks.

Town, Dinner & Fireside

  • For town: Casual clothes, comfortable walking shoes, and a reusable shopping bag.
  • For dinner: Save restaurant details or reservations before leaving reliable service.
  • For the fire: Pick up firewood and s’mores supplies locally, then check current burn restrictions before lighting anything.

Already at the Haven

Fresh linens, bath towels, dedicated hot-tub towels, kitchen basics, and the important stuff are ready for you. Bring less than you think. The mountains will not be grading your outfit.
