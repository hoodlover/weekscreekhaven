Getting here

Navigation is reliable through most of the drive, but service can fade inside the Aska Adventure Area. Load the route in Blue Ridge and keep these final turns handy.

01

Begin in downtown Blue Ridge

Open your preferred map app before leaving town. This is also the best place to pick up groceries or anything you want for the stay.

02

Follow Aska Road south

Take Aska Road out of Blue Ridge, just beyond Ace Hardware. Stay on it through the mountain curves until the road ends at a T.

03

Turn left onto Dial Road

Van Zandt’s Store, a small country store and grill, will be on your right and confirms you are on the correct route.

04

Cross the Toccoa River

After the small bridge, turn right at the next T. Shortly afterward, turn left onto Weeks Creek Road.

05

Stay left at the fork

Continue into the hollow and stay left when the road divides. The cedar “Welcome Friends” sign marks the driveway on your left.

A smooth arrival

Four details that make the transition from mountain road to cabin time a little easier.

Look for this

The driveway sign

The cedar “Welcome Friends” sign marks the driveway. Stay left at the fork immediately before it.

Final approach

Take the curves slowly

The final road is narrow, winding, and gravel in places. There is no need to rush; oncoming traffic is uncommon but possible.

At the cabin

Parking is on site

The level parking area beside the cabin comfortably accommodates three vehicles, with room for a fourth when parked carefully.

Arrival time

Check-in begins at 4 PM

Arriving early? Downtown Blue Ridge, local shops, and the Toccoa River are good places to spend the time before the cabin is ready.
